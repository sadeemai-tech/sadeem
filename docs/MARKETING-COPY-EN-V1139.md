## 5. Feature bullets (honest, benefit-first)
- **Local-first, always.** Your code, keys, and playbook stay on your machine. No proxy, no resold inference, no telemetry.
- **145 tools, actually callable.** Read, edit, run, browse, verify, generate — every tool has a real executor and works by name.
- **It learns from you.** A living DNA + playbook remembers your preferences, corrections, and what worked — and keeps improving across sessions.
- **Subagents with full capability.** Specialist agents with the same 145 tools — click a card to step inside a child session, like a session within a session.
- **Plan, debug, review modes.** Approval gates before risky edits; checkpoints to rewind.
- **Schedules, flows, and MCP.** Cron jobs that really fire, reusable prompt flows, and Model Context Protocol servers.
- **Live preview & verification.** It builds a page, previews it, looks at the screenshot, and fixes what it sees — before it says "done."
- **Bilingual, RTL-first.** Arabic and English, both fully supported.
- **Bring your own keys.** Your provider, your model, your cost. Free season — no subscription.
